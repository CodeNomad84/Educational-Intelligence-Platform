# Parent manual

Who this is: a parent or guardian with an account linked to your child (the school creates it; the first password is on the login sheet you were given). You land on `/students/`.

## First login and your password
**Steps:** log in at `/login/` with the username and temporary password from the school; you are asked to choose your own password. Later: `/account/` → change password. Lost password: ask the school (the school admin resets it; a grade lead may if the school allows).
**Ask:** the school admin.

## See your child's record
**Steps:** `/students/` → your child → "پرونده ۳۶۰".
**You see:** your child's identity, grades per year and subject, an attendance summary, and teacher recommendations that the teacher marked as visible to the student and parents.
**You do not see:** other children; recommendations the teacher kept staff-only; counseling notes; alerts, intervention plans, mission or exam analytics (no parent view exists for them); who referred whom.
**PENDING POLICY:** how much of the individual record a parent should see (open item C5a-R3 / C6 in `docs/SECURITY.md`); consent screens; notifications and parent-teacher messaging are **NOT BUILT**.
**Ask:** the school (class teacher or grade lead) for anything not shown here.
