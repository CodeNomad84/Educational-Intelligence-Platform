# Student manual

Who this is: a student with an account linked to your own record. You land on `/me/`.

## First login and your password
**Steps:** log in at `/login/` with the username and temporary password from the school, then choose your own password. Later: `/account/`. Lost password: ask the school.

## Your page
**Steps:** `/me/`.
**You see:** your own grades and the teacher feedback marked as visible to you.
**You do not see:** your identity details, attendance, anyone else's data, alerts, intervention plans, counseling, mission results (no student view of them exists).

## Practice exams (if the school has the exams module)
**Steps:** `/exams/` → choose an exam → answer one question at a time. A wrong answer turns red and shows why it is wrong; try again until you get it right, then read why the right answer is right. Practice only: it never changes your grades.
**You see:** your own progress. **You do not see:** other students' results or a ranking. Time spent is used only against your own usual pace.
**Ask:** your teacher.

## Study assistant (demo)
**Steps:** `/me/assistant/` (shown locked if the school does not have it). Today it is a rule-based demo; no real AI model is connected and nothing leaves the school's system.
**PENDING POLICY:** the real tutor (external AI provider, safety escalation to adults, transparency to the student) is not built and needs owner decisions.
