# Educational-Intelligence-Platform
An AI-native Educational Intelligence Platform designed for schools to monitor, analyze, and support student growth, engagement, well-being, and educational outcomes.
The platform combines educational analytics, behavioral observations, growth tracking, risk detection, intervention management, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI-powered educational assistants into a unified ecosystem.

Core Capabilities
Student Growth Analytics
Behavioral & Educational Risk Detection
Attendance & Assessment Management
Teacher, Counselor, Parent, and Principal Dashboards
Educational Intervention Planning & Follow-up
Explainable AI & Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support
RAG-based Educational Knowledge Assistant
School Policy & Guidance Assistant
Personalized Parent and Teacher AI Copilots
Educational Data Intelligence & Reporting
Privacy-First and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Architecture
Backend: FastAPI + PostgreSQL
Frontend: React + TypeScript
AI Layer: LLM APIs, RAG, Feature Store, Predictive Models
Vector Database: Qdrant
Infrastructure: Docker, Nginx, CI/CD, Monitoring
Analytics: Growth Engine, Risk Engine, Intervention Engine
Vision

To build an Educational Operating System that helps educators, counselors, school leaders, and families make better decisions through data, educational knowledge, and responsible AI—while keeping humans at the center of the educational process.
